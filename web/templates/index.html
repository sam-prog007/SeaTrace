Current and wind

Dataset values are averaged over the area the boat can reach during the gap. Otherwise leave 0 unless you know the real value (east and north are positive towards the east and the north). The wind pushes the boat downwind by a small share of its speed, on top of the current; the HMM is also shown without this push and with it doubled.
